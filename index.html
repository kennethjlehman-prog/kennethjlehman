ha-nest-app

ha-nest-app is a private, personal-use application that connects one person's Home Assistant installation to their own Google Nest devices through the Google Smart Device Management API.

It is used only to view and control the owner's own Nest devices (such as thermostats and cameras) from within Home Assistant. It is not a public service, is not offered to other users, and does not collect data from anyone else.

See the Privacy Policy for details on how data is handled.
